<aside class="contact-aside">
	<h2 class="contact-aside-title">What happens to a submission</h2>
	<p>Works offered to the archive are scanned, transcribed, and where necessary translated under the archive's <a href="/about/#editorial-method">editorial method</a>, then deposited at the <a href="https://archive.org/">Internet Archive</a> alongside their article here.</p>
	<p>Corrections are read against the scans before anything is changed. If you are reporting an error in a translation, quoting the surrounding sentence and the printed page number helps considerably.</p>
